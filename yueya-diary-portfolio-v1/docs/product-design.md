# Product Design Notes

## 1. User

Primary users:

- working parents of infants;
- families who employ a childcare worker (育儿嫂);
- childcare workers who need a simple and consistent recording method.

## 2. Core Job to Be Done

For parents:

> When I cannot stay with my baby during the day, I want a simple way to understand what happened without constantly messaging the caregiver.

For caregivers:

> I want a clear, lightweight way to record important childcare activities without writing long daily reports.

## 3. Main Product Tension

The product needs to create visibility without making the caregiver feel constantly monitored.

That means the interaction should be:

- structured;
- quick;
- neutral;
- consistent;
- easy to review later.

## 4. Feature Prioritisation

The first version prioritises events that parents most commonly care about:

1. Feeding
2. Sleep
3. Solid food and allergies
4. Bowel movements
5. Mood / temperature
6. Exercise and outdoor activity
7. Reading / music
8. Notes

The product intentionally avoids over-complicating the first version with advanced analytics.

## 5. Emotional Layer

The product is partly an anxiety-reduction tool.

A parent returning to work may not need continuous live monitoring. In many cases, a trustworthy daily record is enough to reduce uncertainty.

This is why the product focuses on visibility and history rather than surveillance.

## 6. Future Product Questions

Future testing should answer:

- Which fields do caregivers find too time-consuming?
- Which records do parents actually review?
- Do parents want daily summaries or raw records?
- How should multiple caregivers be handled?
- What historical comparison is most useful?
- Can the product help with caregiver handover?
